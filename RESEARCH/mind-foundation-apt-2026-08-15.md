## Program facts (their own words, condensed)
- Augmented Psychotherapy Training (APT), MIND Foundation gGmbH, Berlin.
  15 months, hybrid: THREE on-site intensive weeks in Berlin + weekly online
  sessions (lectures, journal club, mentoring with experienced psychedelic
  therapists, meditation training) + self-paced Molecules course and Beyond
  Experience integration workshop.
- Two therapy forms taught: Psychedelic-Augmented Psychotherapy (pharmacological
  e.g. psilocybin AND non-pharmacological induction e.g. breathwork,
  stroboscopic light) and Integration-Focused Psychotherapy (incl. harm
  prevention/mitigation).
- Audience: medical doctors, psychotherapists, other mental health
  professionals. Certificate awarded: "Psychedelic Therapy (Integration-
  Focused and Psychedelic-Augmented Psychotherapy)".
- Cohort 9 (ENGLISH): starts 7 Nov 2026. Intensive weeks: 7–13 Nov 2026;
  3–9 Jul 2027; 19–25 Feb 2028. NOTE: the page contradicts itself once,
  listing week 3 as "2027" in one spot and 2028 in another — 2028 is the
  consistent/sequential reading; verify at build time.
- Cohort 10 (GERMAN): starts 13 Mar 2027 (weeks: 13–19 Mar 2027, 30 Oct–
  5 Nov 2027, 10–16 Jun 2028). Waiting-list enrollment open, rolling
  admissions, limited spots.
- COST: not published on the main page — check /augmented-psychotherapy-
  training/apply and the brochure before writing; the existing site page
  may already carry a verified figure.
- Faculty: MIND Foundation + OVID Clinic Berlin staff + external clinicians/
  researchers. Partner: OVID Clinic (ovid-clinics.com). Scholarships exist
  (/augmented-psychotherapy-training/scholarships). Public list of certified
  APT therapists (/augmented-psychotherapy-training/therapist-list).
- Twelve published "APT Outcomes" (6 basic + 6 practical competencies) —
  full text captured in this fetch; good material for a structured section.
- Monthly public info events (English + German).
- Contact: apt-office@mind-foundation.org, +49 30 2904 7658, Boxhagener
  Str. 78, 10245 Berlin.
