## §4980.43(b)(1) — verbatim substance, all three conditions conjunctive

Postdegree hours gained BEFORE issuance of the associate registration are
credited toward licensure only if ALL of:

- (A) "the board receives the application within 90 days of the granting of
  the qualifying master's degree or doctoral degree" — RECEIVES, not
  postmarked or submitted; and the clock runs from degree GRANTING.
- (B) for graduate study completed on or after 1 Jan 2020: "the experience is
  obtained at a workplace that, prior to the registration applicant gaining
  supervised experience hours, requires completed Live Scan fingerprinting.
  The applicant shall provide the board with a copy of that completed State of
  California 'Request for Live Scan Service' form with the application for
  licensure." — Live Scan must precede the hours, and the FORM must be kept
  and filed later with the licensure application.
- (C) "the board subsequently grants the associate registration."

§4980.43(b)(2): "The applicant shall not be employed or volunteer in a
private practice or a professional corporation until the applicant has been
issued an associate registration by the board." — the gap window excludes
private practice entirely.

## Adjacent verified facts for the same page (§4980.43.3, same session)

- (a) Trainees/associates/applicants work ONLY as employees or volunteers,
  never independent contractors; W-2 copies (or most-recent pay stub for the
  unfinished tax year) filed with the licensure application if employed;
  employer letter verifying volunteer status if volunteering.
- (c)(3) An applicant for associate registration cannot be employed or
  volunteer in a private practice (mirrors 4980.43(b)(2)).
