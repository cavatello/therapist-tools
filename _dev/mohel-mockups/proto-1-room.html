Everyone's here. Nobody's started anything yet.

You cannot tell anybody to do anything. Click the floor to walk. Standing beside someone cools them down and costs you patience — standing between two people who are arguing cools both, and costs double. People drift on their own. Certain pairs should not be left alone together, and you already know which ones. Get the room cool enough to start before the morning gets away from you.
