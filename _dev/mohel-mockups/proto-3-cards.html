Things you can say
You can only say what's in your hand. Two breaths a minute; every card costs one. Each situation wants a certain kind of thing said to it, and gets worse if you leave it — a demand that grows while you deal with somebody else. There are two copies of “Tell them the truth” in the whole deck and it is the only card that solves anything outright. Everything else is management.
