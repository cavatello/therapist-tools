What this programme actually is

OrientationSystemic family therapy inside a CACREP counselor-education frame — no single named model is claimed; faculty listed in the handbook include an AAMFT Approved Supervisor, a Theraplay Trainer/Supervisor, and a supervisor who has run Spanish-language supervision for therapists-in-training.

The first thing a prospective student notices is the name: Fresno State still awards an "M.S. in Marriage, Family and Child Counseling." MFCC was California's licence title until Business and Professions Code § 4980.08 renamed "licensed marriage, family and child counselor" to "licensed marriage and family therapist," operative 1 July 1999. The degree title is legacy, not a defect, and it is not a licensure problem: § 4980.36(b) still lists "marriage, family, and child counseling" first among the qualifying degree titles the BBS accepts, ahead of "marriage and family therapy." When you fill out BBS paperwork the degree name on the transcript will match a title written into the statute. The one practical consequence is search: a program with a 1990s name is easy to miss, and out-of-state boards that pattern-match on "marriage and family therapy" may require the extra sentence of explanation.

The clinic is the reason to look at this program, and it is not on campus. The Fresno Family Counseling Center has operated since 1985 and sits at 5151 N. Palm Ave, Suite 200 — across from Fig Garden Village in northwest Fresno, roughly seven miles from the main campus at 5005 N. Maple. Fresno State News reported it as 4,400 square feet with 14 meeting rooms delivering close to 10,000 therapy sessions a year, in English and Spanish, on a sliding scale ($40–$60 for a first counselling session, $30–$50 after, cash or check only, no insurance and no Medi-Cal), with roughly two-thirds of clients described as economically disadvantaged and unable to be served by the county system. The department's own phrase for how supervision works there is "Dual Channel Feedback": sessions are videotaped and a licensed supervisor watches from a monitoring room and feeds comments in during the session, not afterward. Very few California MFT programs run a clinic at that volume, and fewer still put supervisors on a live channel into the room.

Structurally it is a CACREP counselor-education programme wearing an MFCC label, and it runs on a cohort model that admits twice a year: "Each semester, 18 students will be admitted into a cohort," full-time, taking a designated sequence. Classes are scheduled 1:00–3:50, 4:00–6:50 and 7:00–9:50 — daytime and evening both, so this is not an after-work programme. The degree is 60 units, but nine further units of prerequisites (ERE 153 Educational Statistics, COUN 174, COUN 176 or PSYCH 166) sit outside it and must be no older than ten years with a C or better, and LPCC eligibility costs another 12 units (COUN 270, 271, 272, 273) on top of the 60. California-resident tuition is among the lowest in the state: $5,172.50 per semester full-time (6.1+ units) for Fall 2026, $3,378.50 part-time. One oddity worth knowing before you rely on published outcomes — the MFCC programme's own "Outcomes and Assessment" page reports completion, comps and employment figures for the CRMHC (rehabilitation counselling) programme, not for MFCC.

See it and hear it

The courses that define it

Every California programme teaches law and ethics, assessment and a survey of theories. These are the courses that are not on every list — the ones that tell you what this particular faculty believes therapy is. The quoted text is the catalog’s own; the note underneath each one is mine.

COUN 2384 units

Advanced Practicum in Counseling

"In addition to completing COUN 208, students are required to complete an advanced practicum course held at the Fresno Family Counseling Center. Students who enroll in the COUN 238 practicum receive close supervision as they work directly with individuals, couples, families and children."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingThis is the course the programme is built around, and it is rationed: you apply a full year before you intend to take it, name the semester you want, and if you cannot enrol in the semester you are offered you "forfeit" your priority and go to the back of the waiting list. Admittance depends on the date of your application and a recommendation from your COUN 208 instructor, and everyone admitted attends a three-day mandatory orientation the week before the semester starts. Almost no other California MFT programme makes its in-house clinic a competitive, application-gated course.

COUN 2084 units

Practicum in Counseling

"This course offers supervised, on-campus counseling experiences with selected clients. Students gain experience in individual counseling, critiquing of recorded sessions and typescripts, observations, case consultation and case report writing... This course consists of two hours of seminar and four hours of lab experience."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingThe typescript requirement is the tell — not just watching your recordings but transcribing sessions and being critiqued on the transcript, plus reflection journals and peer critique of classmates' sessions. It is a six-contact-hour course carrying four units, taken in the second semester, and a B or better is required to move on. It also functions as the audition for COUN 238: your 208 instructor's recommendation is part of the advanced-practicum application.

COUN 234a–e6 total units

Sexuality in Human Relationships (1); Violence in Intimate Relationships (1); Substance Abuse Treatment (1); Psychopharmacology (2); Consultation and Recovery (1)

Five separate 1- and 2-unit modules inside the 31-unit MFCC block, listed individually in the degree requirements rather than folded into full semester courses. COUN 271 (Addictions Counseling, 3) substitutes for 234c and COUN 272 (Psychopharmacology, 3) substitutes for 234d. catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingMost California programmes teach the BBS content areas as full three-unit courses; Fresno State breaks five of them into micro-modules, which is why the MFCC block reaches 31 units without feeling like eleven courses. The substitution rule matters if you want the LPCC: taking the three-unit LPCC versions of addictions and psychopharmacology displaces the one- and two-unit modules, so the real marginal cost of LPCC eligibility is smaller than the headline 12 units — and the published cohort roadmap already schedules COUN 272 rather than COUN 234d.

COUN 2396 units

Field Placement in Counseling

"Students in COUN 239 are expected to make a two-semester commitment to a selected field site. The student must complete 300 hours of approved fieldwork hours per semester for a total of 600 hours... 300 of the 600 hours must include providing direct face-to-face counseling services and is required to complete a minimum of 10 hours of experience leading groups."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingThis is the half of the training the school does not run for you: "The student is responsible for selecting a site that will provide the type of counseling experience that is of interest to his/her individual preferences," subject to faculty approval. You need 40 units completed before you can enrol, a signed field placement contract, professional liability insurance, and a permission number. The 10-hour group-leadership floor is a CACREP artifact and is the only sub-minimum the programme publishes — there is no stated relational (couple/family) hour minimum at this stage.

COUN 2203 units

Career Development Theory

Required core course that also carries the university's graduate writing requirement: "Students must pass the graduate writing requirement, which is part of COUN 220, prior to applying for Advancement to Candidacy."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingWorth flagging because it is a hidden gate rather than a distinctive course: fail the writing requirement embedded in the career course and you cannot advance to candidacy, which in turn blocks the culminating experience and graduation. Note that the published cohort roadmap marks COUN 206 (Counseling through the Lifespan) as the GWR course instead — the two documents disagree, so confirm with an adviser which one currently carries it.

How practicum actually works

Californian clinical training runs in two legally distinct stages, and the difference decides what a programme can and cannot do for you.

A trainee is a student. You see clients while still enrolled, at a site your programme approves, supervised by someone the site provides. This is practicum, and it is the stage a programme controls. An associate — registered with the Board after you graduate — is no longer a student, is usually paid, and is working toward the 3,000 hours the licence requires. The Board renamed this stage from “intern” to “associate” in 2018, so any page still saying “MFT intern” is quoting something out of date.

Hours you bank as a trainee count toward the 3,000, up to a limit, which is why the size and quality of a programme’s placement matters long after graduation: a student who leaves with 1,000 hours starts the associate years a third of the way through them, and one who leaves with the 225-hour minimum starts at the beginning.

Practicum is also where a degree most often stops being on schedule. Coursework runs on a calendar the school controls; placement does not. The questions that matter are therefore all about who owns the problem of finding you a site — and the answer differs enormously between programmes on this list.

In-house clinicFresno Family Counseling Center (FFCC), 5151 N. Palm Ave, Suite 200, Fresno — off campus, in operation since 1985

Training runs in three stages and the middle one is the distinctive part. COUN 208 is an on-campus practicum with selected clients: two hours of seminar plus four hours of lab, individual sessions, recorded-session and typescript critique, peer observation and case reports, with a B or better required to continue. COUN 238 then moves you to the Fresno Family Counseling Center, the department's own clinic, where you carry individuals, couples, families and children under what the handbook calls "highly structured, intensely supervised" conditions — sessions are videotaped and a licensed supervisor observes from a monitoring room and feeds comments in live ("Dual Channel Feedback"). Access to COUN 238 is application-gated: you apply a year ahead by emailing a form to the clinic, you must already have COUN 208, 230 and 232 (with 231 concurrent), admittance turns on application date, instructor recommendation and availability, and everyone admitted attends a three-day mandatory orientation the week before the term. COUN 239 is the opposite arrangement: the student picks the community site, the supervising faculty member must approve it, you need 40 units completed and professional liability insurance, and you sign a field placement contract with the site, the university and the off-campus supervisor. You commit to one site for two semesters, log 300 hours a semester, and the off-campus supervisor writes the evaluation while a university supervisor clears the file. Beyond the 10-hour group-leadership floor there is no published relational sub-minimum; the BBS's own relational-hour rules apply to hours you want to count toward licensure. source ↗

Will this degree travel

Not COAMFTE accredited

That is not a defect — 53 of the 65 institutions on the Board’s list are in the same position, including most of the Cal States, and their graduates license in California every year.

It becomes a problem if you leave. Several states will not accept a non-COAMFTE degree without remedial coursework, and a few will not accept it at all. If there is any chance you move, check the destination board’s rules before you enrol, not after.

What to ask before you apply

None of these are answered on a programme page, and all five change what the degree is actually worth to you.

Where do students actually get placed, and who finds the placement?

The single question that separates programmes. Ask for a list of current sites, and ask plainly whether the programme places you or expects you to find your own. A rural or online student unable to find a site cannot graduate.

How many of your graduates passed the clinical exam last year?

The Board publishes this by school, so the programme cannot be the only source. Ask anyway, and see whether the answer matches.

What is the total cost, including fees, in the year I would start?

Per-unit rates move. Multiply by the actual unit count and add campus fees before comparing anything.

How many relational hours does a typical student leave with?

The 500 couples-and-families hours are carved out of the 1,750 clinical ones, and they are the category associates most often finish short on. A programme placing students in adult individual settings will not generate them.

Does this degree also make me eligible for the LPCC?

Sometimes yes, sometimes with extra units, sometimes only in one delivery format. Get it in writing rather than from a brochure.

What I could not establish

Everything above is sourced. These are the things I looked for and could not establish - take them to admissions, and note how quickly and precisely they answer.

  • The Fresno State academic catalog (catalog.fresnostate.edu) sits behind an AWS WAF bot challenge and could not be fetched, so official catalog course descriptions for the COUN courses are missing. The course titles, codes and unit values here come from the department's advising sheet, student handbook and curriculum page; the only full course description obtained is the COUN 208 syllabus.
  • No total client-contact-hour figure is published for COUN 238, the clinic practicum — only that supervision is close and sessions are videotaped.
  • No relational (couple/family) hour sub-minimum is published for any stage of training.
  • The programme's "Outcomes and Assessment" page carries a CACREP statement for MFCC but then reports student outcomes for the CRMHC (Clinical Rehabilitation and Mental Health Counseling) programme — enrolment, completion, comps pass rate and employment figures are all labelled CRMHC. No MFCC-specific outcome data was found.
  • The student handbook says the graduate writing requirement sits inside COUN 220; the cohort roadmap marks COUN 206 as the GWR course. The documents disagree.
  • The cohort roadmaps are dated 10/10/2020 and the advising sheet 11/1/2019; no newer sequence document is published.
  • Nothing credible was found in student discussion forums about this programme. Reddit's search endpoints were unreachable during this pass, and an earlier survey of r/therapists, r/askatherapist, r/psychologystudents, The GradCafe and Student Doctor Network found no Fresno State MFCC thread worth citing.
