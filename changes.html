Changelog

What changed, and when.

The figures on this site that have moved, most recent first, each with the page it affects and the source it came from.

12 entriesUpdated 6 August 2026

A log of numbers that moved — fees, limits, rates, panel status, accreditation actions — not a list of pages that were published. On a site whose whole claim is that the figures are checked, this is the proof of the claim.

Every entry links to the page that carries the number and, where one exists, to the primary source. If you spot something that has moved and is not here, tell me.

  1. 2026-08-07

    Reconciled the programme directory against the Board's own list: 13 institutions added (65 to 78), and the LPCC column corrected for 33 of them — 7 that showed no answer are listed for LMFT and not LPCC, including Cal Poly Humboldt, Cal Poly San Luis Obispo and USC.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  2. 2026-08-07

    Added the Board's Notice to Students about Sentio University: its graduates may register as Associates but cannot sit the Clinical Exam or be licensed until Sentio is fully accredited, and if that does not happen by July 2028 existing Associates are disqualified and their hours void.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  3. 2026-07-01

    BBS fees halved: registration through licence fell from $1,750 to $875, reverting on 30 June 2030.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  4. 2026-06-01

    Evernorth (Cigna) behavioral health closed to new individual applicants, pointing providers back after 1 September 2026.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  5. 2026-01-01

    California SDI withholding set at 1.3% for 2026, on the whole salary with no ceiling — $1,248 on a $96,000 S-corp wage.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  6. 2026-01-01

    The 401(k) employee deferral rose to $24,500 and the IRA limit to $7,500, $8,600 from age 50.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  7. 2026-01-01

    The SIMPLE IRA deferral rose from $16,500 to $17,000, with the catch-up at $4,000.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  8. 2026-01-01

    Direct Roth contributions phase out between $153,000 and $168,000 of modified AGI filing single, and $242,000 to $252,000 jointly.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  9. 2025-07-01

    UC Berkeley's psychedelic facilitation certificate paused enrolment for 2025-26; its pages have not been updated since, and no restart or tuition has been announced.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  10. 2025-01-01

    Oregon's residency requirement for psilocybin facilitator licensure expired, so a Californian may now hold one — while still facilitating only inside an Oregon service centre.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  11. 2024-12-01

    The psilocybin facilitator training left Naropa University for the independent Memoru Center, which is Colorado-approved only and has dropped Oregon.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
  12. 2024-01-01

    California removed the SDI wage cap, making all wages subject to contributions — which is what turns an S-corp salary into an uncapped 1.3% charge.

    the page that carries it → source ↗
