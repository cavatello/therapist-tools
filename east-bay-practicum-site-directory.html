What this list is, and is not

One chapter’s list, annotated. Not the whole market.

This is a curated, named-site directory, and it is not exhaustive. It reprints one source: East Bay CAMFT’s public Practicum Site Directory for the 2026–27 training year, read 15 August 2026. A chapter directory lists the sites that chose to file an entry with the chapter — nothing about the sites that did not. The full universe of settings a trainee may lawfully work in is the companion page, the Bay Area practicum settings directory.

The frame is the trainee’s, and the law draws it: a practicum student may not work in a private practice or a professional corporation at all — BPC §4980.43.3(b). Three entries below say they also hire registered associates; that fact matters for a different reader at a different stage, and it is broken out separately so nobody mistakes an associate job for a practicum seat.

Every acceptance status below is the directory’s own statement at the 15 August 2026 read. The chapter’s page is live and filterable and will drift; re-check it before contacting anyone. One count to have straight: the directory’s own counter said “Showing all 22 sites” while 21 entries carried published details at that read — this page prints the 21.

A different reader, a different stage

The three entries that hire registered associates.

A practicum seat and an associate job are different legal objects: the trainee bar on private practice — BPC §4980.43.3(b) — lifts only once the associate registration is issued. Three of the 21 entries state, in the directory itself at the 15 Aug 2026 read, that they hire registered associates. The wider associate market is its own directory, and the clock rules are on the 90-day rule page.

The order that saves you cycles

How to use a curated list.

1Read the MOU line before the status line.

A site whose MOU list names your school is weeks closer than an identical site that would need a new agreement — BPC §4980.42(e) puts the written site agreement on the school, and an existing MOU means that work may be done.

2Treat every status as dated, because it is.

Every acceptance value on this page carries the 15 August 2026 read date. The chapter’s page is live and filterable; re-read the directory itself before writing to a contact, and expect drift — a directory that was honest in August is not a promise about January.

3Widen the search past the chapter’s list.

This page is curated, not complete. The lawful universe is on the settings directory, and the method page walks the search itself — whose job the placement is at your kind of program, and the questions that sort sites quickly.

4Check the statute frame before anything else convinces you.

Employee or volunteer, never a 1099; no private practice for trainees at all; enrollment and the school’s agreement before hours count. The full trainee rules are on the practicum page.
