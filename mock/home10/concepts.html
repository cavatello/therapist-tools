Computed for a California-resident sole proprietor filing single at 2026 federal and California rates, with no other household income. Your own figure will differ; the calculator below runs it on your numbers.

A California therapist cannot form an LLC.

Cal. Corp. Code §17701.04(e) blocks a limited liability company from rendering services that require a licence. The real choice is sole proprietorship or a professional corporation.

§17701.04(e)the section that blocks it
What you should know about this figure

The professional corporation is a state filing; the S-corp election is a separate federal tax choice made on top of it. The two are routinely conflated.

California removed the SDI wage ceiling.

State disability insurance is charged at 1.3% on the entire salary with no cap — $1,248 on a $96,000 S-corp wage. Most S-corp comparisons omit it entirely.

$1,248on a $96,000 salary
What you should know about this figure

Rate confirmed against the EDD schedule for 2026. The cap was removed in 2024, which is why older comparisons understate the cost of an S-corp in California.

In short

A California therapist has two options, not three. An LLC is not available — Cal. Corp. Code §17701.04(e) blocks it for licensed services — so the choice is sole proprietorship or a professional corporation electing S-corp treatment.

On a $208,350 profit the corporation nets about $6,912 more, after the $800 franchise tax and payroll costs. That advantage shrinks with profit and can reverse below roughly $80,000, and California's uncapped 1.3% SDI charge takes $1,248 of it back on a $96,000 salary.

If you read nothing else: the entity is a smaller lever than the retirement account, and most people get that backwards.
