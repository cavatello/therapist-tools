Why it is free

Because the arithmetic should be.

One person, working in California, building the tools they wanted and could not find. Every figure traces to a published rate, limit or statute, and every page carries the date it was last checked — not the date it was written, which tells you nothing.

  • It computes, it doesn't opineEvery figure is arithmetic on a published rate, limit or statute — not a range someone remembered.
  • California specificallySDI, the franchise tax, the BBS schedule, §17701.04(e). The parts that make California different are the parts that cost money.
  • Nothing saved, no accountEvery calculator runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Stamped checked, not publishedA correct page dated 2024 looks worse than a wrong page dated last week. Every page carries the date it was last verified.
