Who this is for

California therapists, at every stage — somebody choosing between an MFT and a doctorate, an associate counting toward 3,000 hours, a newly licensed clinician working out what a practice actually pays, and a practice with room to grow. Everything here is free, and nothing asks you to make an account. Every figure is either computed from your own numbers or cited to the rule it came from, and every page carries the month it was last checked.

If you do not know where to start, the three routes below are the same material sorted three ways: by the number you need, by the question you arrived with, or by the area you are dealing with.

What moved, and when

This site is a reference, so the useful kind of recency is not “what did you publish this week” — it is which numbers have changed under you. Every page carries the month it was last checked. These are the figures that actually moved.

  • 2026-08-08Added a directory of every PsyD program based in California, grouped by APA accreditation status. The organising fact: the Board of Psychology requires regional institutional accreditation, not APA programmatic accreditation, but 16 CCR 1387 requires an APA/APPIC/CAPIC internship - so a non-APA doctorate can license you here and still make the internship year and any move out of state much harder.what it changes →
  • 2026-08-07Reconciled the program directory against the Board's own list: 13 institutions added (65 to 78), and the LPCC column corrected for 33 of them — 7 that showed no answer are listed for LMFT and not LPCC, including Cal Poly Humboldt, Cal Poly San Luis Obispo and USC.what it changes →
  • 2026-08-07Added the Board's Notice to Students about Sentio University: its graduates may register as Associates but cannot sit the Clinical Exam or be licensed until Sentio is fully accredited, and if that does not happen by July 2028 existing Associates are disqualified and their hours void.what it changes →
  • 2026-07-01BBS fees halved: registration through license fell from $1,750 to $875, reverting on 30 June 2030.what it changes →
