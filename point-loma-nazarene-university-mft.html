What this program actually is

OrientationGeneralist and eclectic — a broad theories survey aimed squarely at BBS licensure, carrying a three-course Christian faith-integration thread that the program itself says is not core

The first thing to correct is a common misreading of this program: PLNU's M.A. in Clinical Counseling does not have separate MFT and PCC emphases with different unit counts. There is one 63-unit curriculum, and the program's own page states it flatly — "All students take the 63 program units, regardless of the license they hope to receive." The catalog's "63-65" range is not a second track; it is the optional PSY 6098 Practicum Extension (0-2 units), which exists so students who finish the three required practicum courses early can stay in trainee status and keep accruing licensure hours while they finish the degree. Every graduate leaves eligible to register with the BBS as an associate on either the LMFT or the LPCC path and picks which one to pursue afterwards. The only real fork in the program is on-ground versus online, and that fork is more consequential than the license question.

On the Christian-institution question, PLNU answers it itself, in writing, in its own FAQ: "PLNU is a Nazarene school and our professors are Christian, but this program is not following a Christian curriculum... There are three classes where faith integration is discussed, but it is not a core component of the curriculum. Faith is addressed as a whole and not only from the Christian traditions." That is a fair description of the course list. Faith appears in exactly three of twenty-one courses — PSY 6001 Spiritual Integration in Clinical Practice, and the two-semester required theory sequence PSY 6025/6026 Counseling Theories and Faith Integration I and II — nine units out of sixty-three. It is worth noticing where those nine units sit, though: most Christian programs put integration in a standalone bolt-on course. PLNU put it in the title of the required theories sequence, so the orientation you graduate with is developed alongside a faith-integration frame rather than next to it. There is no Bible, theology or ministry requirement, and no doctrinal statement in the admissions checklist.

What actually distinguishes the program is the clinical spine. PLNU runs an on-site training clinic at its Mission Valley Regional Center in partnership with the Center for Enriching Relationships, and on-ground students see a real client there — recorded — before they are cleared for an outside placement. The graduation bar is 700 total supervised hours with 300 face-to-face direct-service hours, which is roughly double what several other California master's programs require and is the single hardest number in this program to plan around. It is also, deliberately, a small operation: two cohorts of an estimated 15-20, one meeting Monday/Wednesday and one Tuesday/Thursday, at a satellite center in Mission Valley — not on the Point Loma campus. The FA26 on-ground cohort was already closed as of this writing, with SP27 the next on-ground start.

See it and hear it

The courses that define it

Every California program teaches law and ethics, assessment and a survey of theories. These are the courses that are not on every list — the ones that tell you what this particular faculty believes therapy is. The quoted text is the catalog’s own; the note underneath each one is mine.

PSY 60940 units

Pre-Practicum (on-ground program)

"Prior to practicum assignments in various approved counseling centers, hospitals, clinics, and social service agencies, all students are required to be observed as they counsel at least one client at the onsite counseling center. Digital recordings of therapy sessions will be utilized to improve therapeutic skills, promote self-awareness, and an understanding of how people change."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingThis is the gate. Before you can enroll in PSY 6095 you must have counseled a real client at PLNU's own clinic, on camera, and been signed off by the Director of Clinical Training. Very few California master's programs put a live recorded client in front of a student inside the school's own clinic before the outside placement — most send you straight to an agency and let the agency be your first client contact.

PSY 61940 units

Pre-Practicum (online program)

"Prior to practicum assignments students will learn basic skills that are required in most placements (e.g., note-taking, treatment planning). Students will have the opportunity to receive formative feedback as they counsel a client using a simulated patient... This class will prepare students for the Objective Simulated Clinical Exam."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingRead this next to PSY 6094 and you have the honest difference between PLNU's two formats. On-ground students rehearse on a real client in the school's clinic; online students rehearse on a standardized simulated patient and sit an OSCE. Same degree, same 63 units, materially different first clinical experience — and this is not stated anywhere in the marketing comparison table.

PSY 6025 / PSY 60263 + 3 units

Counseling Theories and Faith Integration I and II

I: "Major counseling theories and techniques are examined and applied in the context of the helping relationship." II: "Continuing exploration of psychological therapies and techniques with emphasis on the adoption of a theoretical orientation for explaining the etiology and treatment of various mental disorders."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingTwo things at once. It is a full year on theory with an explicit requirement that you land on your own orientation by the end — more than most programs demand — and it is where PLNU's faith commitment actually lives, inside the required theory sequence rather than in a separate elective. If you want a wholly secular training this is the course you cannot opt out of.

PSY 60013 units

Spiritual Integration in Clinical Practice

"This course prepares students to integrate spirituality as part of treatment with special emphasis on ethics, Christian theology, and use of self."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingThe one course where Christian theology is named as content rather than framing. It is three units of sixty-three, and it is the concrete answer to 'how Christian is this program' — one required course plus the faith thread in the theories sequence.

PSY 60930 units

Personal Therapy Requirement

Twenty hours of personal psychotherapy from a licensed clinician, completed before graduation. "Two hours of group therapy or one hour of individual therapy counts as one hour of credit toward the requirement. At least ten of the twenty hours must be individual or group therapy and up to ten hours can be marital or family therapy."catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingA required own-therapy hour count is not universal in California, and the accounting rules here are unusually precise — group counts at half rate, and your own couples work is capped at half the requirement. Budget for it: twenty hours of therapy you pay for out of pocket on top of tuition.

PSY 60990 units

Comprehensive Examination

"As a final assessment of competency, each student must pass the Comprehensive Examination, a computerized mock license exam that reflects program coursework and obtained clinical knowledge." Administered during PSY 6097 (in-person) or PSY 6197 (online).catalog ↗

Why it is worth noticingThe exit requirement is not a thesis or a portfolio — it is a simulated BBS licensing exam you must pass to graduate. That tells you what the program optimises for, and it matches the program page's claim that "every course prepares you to take the licensure exam."

How practicum actually works

Californian clinical training runs in two legally distinct stages, and the difference decides what a program can and cannot do for you.

A trainee is a student. You see clients while still enrolled, at a site your program approves, supervised by someone the site provides. This is practicum, and it is the stage a program controls. An associate — registered with the Board after you graduate — is no longer a student, is usually paid, and is working toward the 3,000 hours the license requires. The Board renamed this stage from “intern” to “associate” in 2018, so any page still saying “MFT intern” is quoting something out of date.

Hours you bank as a trainee count toward the 3,000, up to a limit, which is why the size and quality of a program’s placement matters long after graduation: a student who leaves with 1,000 hours starts the associate years a third of the way through them, and one who leaves with the 225-hour minimum starts at the beginning.

Practicum is also where a degree most often stops being on schedule. Coursework runs on a calendar the school controls; placement does not. The questions that matter are therefore all about who owns the problem of finding you a site — and the answer differs enormously between programs on this list.

You apply, with the school’s help

There is an approved-site list and real support, and the seat is still yours to win. Most programs are here and most students manage it. Ask for last year’s numbers rather than the policy: how many students placed on the first round, and how many had to extend.

PLNU will work with you to acquire a practicum experience and we have an on-site clinic where you will engage in hands-on therapeutic practices while being supervised by a licensed professional prior to starting a full practicum placement in the community. While PLNU helps students get matched, it is ultimately the student's responsibility to connect with, interview for, and solidify their placement. the school’s own page →

The school runs its own training clinic.  PLNU trainee practicum clinic (on-campus, Mission Valley) Owning a clinic and holding a seat in it are different things, so this sits beside the model above rather than replacing it.

StartsClinical contact starts in year one at PLNU's own clinic (PSY 6094); the outside traineeship runs across the whole second year, in three consecutive terms (PSY 6095/6096/6097). Spring-start students run a 32-month track so practicum lands correctly, and take only practicum units in their final two semesters.
Hours required700 total hours of supervised training, of which 300 must be face-to-face direct service, accrued across the three-course practicum sequence. No separate relational (couples/family) hour minimum is published for graduation.
In-house clinicOn-site training clinic at the Mission Valley Regional Center, run in partnership with the Center for Enriching Relationships

PLNU's model is clinic-first, agency-second, and the catalog is explicit about the order: "Before placement at an external agency, students will provide psychotherapy to one or more clients at the on-site training clinic, the Center for Enriching Relationships. Upon successful completion of this initial experience and approval by the training staff, students will secure a practicum site in the community and complete a 10-12 month traineeship." That first clinic experience is PSY 6094, a zero-unit gate: you must be observed counseling at least one real client, sessions are digitally recorded and reviewed, and the Director of Clinical Training signs off before you may enroll in PSY 6095. Six named courses (PSY 6011, 6021, 6025, 6029, 6045 and 6094) are prerequisites to that first practicum course, so the whole first year is functionally a run-up. Placement is a shared job rather than an assignment: the program says it maintains "established practicum sites at all the major mental health service providers throughout San Diego" and provides "a high level of support in helping you match to the practicum site that fits you best," but the catalog's own wording is that "students will secure a practicum site in the community" — the school approves and helps, you apply. Expect about 20 hours a week on site in year two on top of roughly 10 hours of class, which is why the program's own FAQ says many students stop working during the second year. The handoff to associate status is clean and stated: pre-degree trainee hours count toward MFT licensure (up to the BBS cap) but not toward LPCC, PSY 6098 lets you keep accruing hours if you finish the sequence early, and on graduation you apply to the BBS for associate registration and continue accruing under supervision for at least 104 weeks. source ↗

Will this degree travel

Not COAMFTE accredited

That is not a defect — 66 of the 78 institutions on the Board’s list are in the same position, including most of the Cal States, and their graduates license in California every year.

It becomes a problem if you leave. Several states will not accept a non-COAMFTE degree without remedial coursework, and a few will not accept it at all. If there is any chance you move, check the destination board’s rules before you enroll, not after.

Counting every attempt rather than only first attempts, the figure is 95% across 75 sittings here, against 66% statewide. First attempts are the fairer comparison — counting resits measures how many tries people needed rather than how many passed, and it penalises a school whose graduates keep going.

On the separate California Law and Ethics exam, 84% of this school’s 166 first-time candidates passed, against 80% statewide.

Recorded by the Board as POINT LOMA NAZARENE UNIVERSITY. Its list is kept by institution and its names lag: a school that has since renamed appears under the name it had when the candidates sat.

What this number is, and what it is not

It describes candidates, not teaching. In 2022 the Council on Social Work Education removed licensing-exam pass rates from its accreditation standards, saying the data “may not be an equitable measure of program outcomes” — after the social-work exam board’s own analysis found first-time pass rates of 84% for white candidates and 45% for Black candidates. A program that enrolls more career-changers and more students of color will post a lower rate while teaching at least as well. The accrediting body with the most reason to want this metric examined it and stopped using it.

The denominator is not this year’s students. These are people who sat the exam during the window, often several years after graduating and sometimes after the program changed shape or owner.

It is published here because it is a real, official figure that is otherwise almost impossible to find — and it is published on this page only. There is no pass-rate column in the directory, nothing is sorted by it, and no school on this site is ranked against another.

What to ask before you apply

None of these are answered on a program page, and all five change what the degree is actually worth to you.

Where do students actually get placed, and who finds the placement?

The single question that separates programs. Ask for a list of current sites, and ask plainly whether the program places you or expects you to find your own. A rural or online student unable to find a site cannot graduate.

How many of your graduates passed the clinical exam last year?

The Board publishes this by school, so the program cannot be the only source. Ask anyway, and see whether the answer matches.

What is the total cost, including fees, in the year I would start?

Per-unit rates move. Multiply by the actual unit count and add campus fees before comparing anything.

How many relational hours does a typical student leave with?

The 500 couples-and-families hours are carved out of the 1,750 clinical ones, and they are the category associates most often finish short on. A program placing students in adult individual settings will not generate them.

Does this degree also make me eligible for the LPCC?

Sometimes yes, sometimes with extra units, sometimes only in one delivery format. Get it in writing rather than from a brochure.

What I could not establish

Everything above is sourced. These are the things I looked for and could not establish - take them to admissions, and note how quickly and precisely they answer.

  • The premise that the MACC has MFT and PCC emphases with different unit counts is wrong: there is one 63-unit curriculum and all students graduate eligible for both licenses. The only unit variation (63-65) is the optional PSY 6098 Practicum Extension.
  • PLNU publishes no term-by-term course sequence for the MACC — only the full required list and the two-year/32-month framing.
  • No minimum relational (couples/family) client-hour count is published for graduation; only the 700-total/300-face-to-face figures.
  • No supervision mechanics are published — individual vs group supervision ratios, whether sessions are live-observed or one-way-mirror, and how many hours of supervision per week are all absent from public pages. Digital recording is confirmed only for the pre-practicum clinic experience.
  • No independent student discussion of this program was found on Reddit, Student Doctor Network or GradCafe. The only student voices available are testimonials PLNU itself publishes.
  • PLNU does not publish BBS licensing-exam pass rates for its graduates.
  • Total program cost is not published as a figure; $848 per unit × 63 units is the tuition base for 2026-27, before fees, the 20 required personal-therapy hours, and the $125-style exam and background-check costs.
