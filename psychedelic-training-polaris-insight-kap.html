Polaris/Rainfall Certificate of Training in Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy

What it is

Polaris Insight Center opened in 2018 as one of the first ketamine-assisted psychotherapy clinics, started training clinicians in 2020, and now says it has trained over 2,000. Its founders are MAPS-trained clinicians who worked in Phase 2 and Phase 3 MDMA-assisted psychotherapy trials for PTSD, and the lead trainers studied with the Ketamine Training Center and KRIYA Institute. That lineage matters mainly because it explains the house style: transpersonal, inner-directed, psychedelic-therapy-flavoured rather than protocol-and-checklist medical.

The structure is deliberately modular rather than a single tuition bill. Everything is priced per unit: each full-day (8-hour) module is $375 ($350 plus a $25 non-refundable admin fee), with 8 CE credits available for an extra $79. Module 1, 'Introduction to Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy', is the prerequisite for Modules 2–5 and the Practice Module. Buying all six as the Foundational Series is $1,950, a $300 discount. An alternative on-ramp is the 2026 Cohort — 52 training hours over five months (12 three-hour sessions, a Training & Practice Module, and four discussion groups) starting 11 September 2026 for $2,450, with 44 CE hours available for a flat $350. The cohort covers the same content as Modules 1–5 but in a fixed group, and its discussion groups count for 3 of the 12 group-consultation hours the certificate requires.

The certificate itself is the real commitment and Polaris is honest that it is 'expected to be a 1-to-2-year process.' It requires: Modules 1–5 plus the Practice Module; any two of nine advanced or special-topic modules; 12 group consultation sessions AND 12 individual consultation sessions, with letters of recommendation from both consultants; one five-day experiential practicum retreat (or an approved alternative such as Grof Transpersonal Training or holotropic breathwork intensives); a minimum of 18 real KAP sessions with at least three different clients; a 3–5 page reflection essay; and an administrative processing fee. That clinical-hours requirement is the thing to plan around: you cannot finish this certificate without already having a working KAP practice, which means you must have solved the prescriber problem first.

The experiential practicum retreat is where trainees have their own ketamine sessions. Polaris does not say so in one blunt sentence, but the retreat page promises you will 'learn through direct experience about the medical, biological, psychological, transpersonal, and phenomenological aspects of the ketamine experience,' screens applicants for medical contraindications (sleep apnea, untreated blood pressure or heart conditions, history of psychosis, stroke risk, active mania, recent TBI, untreated seizure disorders), and is run in collaboration with a medical clinic, Rainfall Medicine. Trainee testimonials on Polaris's own site refer to 'my own journey content' and to 'sitting with someone experiencing a journey.' The retreat price is not published anywhere on the site, which is the single biggest hole in an otherwise transparent price list.

What it lets you do

This is the section the programme’s own marketing is least specific about, so it runs before everything else. It was established against the programme’s own materials and against the two state registers that matter — Oregon’s approved-curriculum list and Colorado’s.

This does not change your licence or your scope of practiceIt is a credential from a school, not from a regulator. California’s Board of Behavioral Sciences neither recognises nor requires it, and holding it adds nothing to what you may lawfully do as an LMFT in this state.
With this you can
  • Practise ketamine-assisted psychotherapy in California today, as the psychotherapy half of a collaborative pair — because ketamine is an FDA-approved anaesthetic that a licensed prescriber can prescribe off-label, this is the one psychedelic-assisted modality a California LMFT/LCSW/LPCC/psychologist can actually do now.
  • Earn CE hours that count toward California licence renewal. Polaris states verbatim: 'Polaris Insight Center is approved by the American Psychological Association to sponsor continuing education for psychologists... The California Board of Behavioral Sciences accepts CE credits for LCSW, LPCC, LEP, and LMFT license renewal for programs offered by approved sponsors of CE by the American Psychological Association.' 8 CE per full-day module ($79), 44 CE for the cohort ($350). Note: not CME, so physicians must check their own board.
  • Be listed in the Polaris/Rainfall KAP Practitioner Directory on completing the certificate — a marketing and referral asset, not a credential.
  • Present a training credential that KAP platforms and clinics actually recognise: Journey Clinical lists Polaris among the didactic trainings it accepts for onboarding (alongside CIIS, MAPS, Fluence, Alchemy and others).
  • Do preparation and integration work with clients around psychedelic experiences that occur outside your office — legal in California with no special credential, and something the Integration and Ethics modules address directly.
You still cannot
  • Prescribe, dispense, possess, or administer ketamine. You are not a prescriber. In the standard KAP arrangement an MD, DO, NP or PA holds all medical authority — clearance, dosing, prescribing, monitoring — and the therapist leads psychotherapy only. Journey Clinical puts the boundary plainly: 'Strict adherence to scope of practice is essential — psychotherapists should never engage in activities that could be interpreted as practicing medicine or psychiatry.'
  • Touch psilocybin, MDMA or LSD. Psilocybin and LSD are Schedule I in California under Health & Safety Code §11054; MDMA-assisted therapy is still investigational and not FDA-approved (Lykos Therapeutics, whose expanded-access programme is fully enrolled and closed, describes itself as still 'focused on pursuing FDA approval').
  • Qualify for Oregon psilocybin facilitator licensure. Polaris Insight Center does not appear on Oregon Psilocybin Services' list of Current Active Psilocybin Training Programs (15 programs; checked 2026-08-06).
  • Qualify for Colorado natural-medicine facilitator licensure. Polaris does not appear on Colorado DORA's Approved Natural Medicine Training Programs list (checked 2026-08-06).
  • Change your BBS licence, title, or scope. The certificate is a credential from a private clinic; the BBS neither recognises nor requires it, and nothing about the training expands what your licence permits.
  • Finish the certificate without a live KAP caseload. The 18-sessions-with-3-clients requirement means you must already have a prescriber partner and paying clients before you can complete it.
CE credits for psychologists are provided by Polaris Insight Center which is sponsoring this program. Polaris Insight Center is approved by the American Psychological Association to sponsor continuing education for psychologists. Polaris Insight Center maintains responsibility for this program and its content. The California Board of Behavioral Sciences accepts CE credits for LCSW, LPCC, LEP, and LMFT license renewal for programs offered by approved sponsors of CE by the American Psychological Association. LCSW, LPCC, LEP, and LMFTs, and other mental health professionals from states other than California need to check with their state licensing board as to whether or not they accept programs offered by approved sponsors of CE by the American Psychological Association.— Polaris Insight Center (with Rainfall Medicine), in its own materials

Cost, length and who can apply

CostThere is no single certificate price; Polaris sells the training à la carte. Published prices, verified 2026-08-06: Foundational Series (Modules 1–5 + Training & Practice Module) $1,950 — 'a $300 discount ($50 off per module)'. Individual full-day module $375 ($350 + $25 non-refundable admin fee), plus $79 for 8 CE credits. Half-day modules (Music, ACT) $215 ($190 + $25), plus $39 for 4 CE credits. Healing the Healer $250 ($225 + $25), plus $49 for 6 CE credits. Rainfall Medical Module $415 ($390 + $25), no CE available. 2026 Cohort (52 hours over 5 months) $2,450 regular tuition with sliding scale; optional CE $350 for 44 CE hours; international tiered pricing $490–$1,715. Consultation groups $900 for 6 months (individual listed groups show $750 for 5 sessions or $900 for 6 sessions). NOT published anywhere: the five-day practicum retreat fee, the individual-consultation fee, and the certificate administrative processing fee — so a full certificate total cannot be computed from public information.
LengthSelf-paced. Polaris: 'The Polaris/Rainfall Certificate of Training in KAP is expected to be a 1-to-2-year process for most individuals.' The 2026 Cohort on-ramp is 5 months; a single module is one day.
HoursFull-day modules are 8 hours each (9am–5pm Pacific), 8 CE credits. Half-day modules (Music, ACT) 4 CE credits; Healing the Healer 6 CE credits. Foundational Series = six modules ≈ 48 hours. 2026 Cohort = 52 training hours over 5 months (12 × 3-hour sessions + one 8-hour Training & Practice Module + 4 × 2-hour discussion groups), 44 CE hours. Certificate adds 12 group + 12 individual consultation sessions, a five-day retreat, and a minimum of 18 KAP sessions with at least 3 clients.
FormatLive online Zoom webinars for all didactic modules (no on-demand). Five-day in-person experiential practicum retreats run with Rainfall Medicine. Individual and group consultation over Zoom, in 6-month and 12-month cohorts.
WhereSan Francisco, CA — live-online modules plus five-day in-person practicum retreats (2026 retreats in Berkeley, CA and Portland, OR)
Who can applyModules are open to 'physicians, psychologists, therapists, nurses, chaplains, and other healthcare professionals'; the Ethics and Healing the Healer modules require no prior KAP training, and Polaris notes some teachings are 'appropriate for healthcare professionals as well as the general public.' The certificate is restricted to licensed clinicians — 'LMFT, LCSW, PsyD, PhD, RN, NP, MD, ND, DO, PCC, LMHC, chaplains, etc.' Pre-licensed clinicians (which in California means associates — AMFT, ASW, APCC) may take the training and participate in the certificate program, but 'will need to be licensed prior to receiving the Polaris/Rainfall KAP Practitioner Training Certificate.' Module 1 is a prerequisite for Modules 2–5 and the Practice Module; it can be waived if you have equivalent introductory KAP training elsewhere, by emailing training@polarisinsight.com. The practicum retreat requires Module 1 or another Polaris module, and screens for medical contraindications.

What you would study

  1. Module 1: Introduction to Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy

    Prerequisite for Modules 2–5 and the Practice Module. 8 hours, $375 (+$79 for 8 CE). Learning objectives: describe dosing strategies and routes of administration; explain the difference between infusion treatment and KAP and the pros and cons of each; identify ketamine side effects and management; summarise the three main elements of the KAP protocol (preparation, experiential, integration); differentiate psycholytic from NOSC treatment processes; discuss the intake process for KAP candidates. Instructors: Harvey Schwartz PhD, Veronika Gold LMFT, Eric Sienknecht PsyD, Robert Voloshin DO. 2026 dates listed: 10 September and 5 November 2026.

What I could not establish

These are the things I looked for and could not establish. Take them to the programme, and notice how quickly and precisely they answer.

  • The five-day practicum retreat fee is not published anywhere on either polarisinsight.com or training.polarisinsight.com — and it is a required certificate component. Store pages exist for a retreat application fee and scholarship balances but list no amounts. This is the single largest unknown in the total cost.
  • The individual-consultation rate is not published; only the group rate ($900/6 months) is. The certificate requires 12 individual sessions.
  • The certificate 'Administrative Processing Fee' amount is not published.
  • Polaris does not state in plain words that trainees receive ketamine at the practicum retreat. The inference here rests on the retreat's own promise of learning 'through direct experience about the medical, biological, psychological, transpersonal, and phenomenological aspects of the ketamine experience', the medical contraindication screening, the Rainfall Medicine (medical clinic) partnership, and participant testimonials referring to 'my own journey content'. Confirm directly with training@polarisinsight.com before assuming.
  • Whether the practicum retreat cost includes lodging and meals is not stated.
  • No independent third-party reviews, journalism, or forum discussion of Polaris training could be retrieved in this environment (search engines and Reddit were blocked). All qualitative material here is either program-published or from Journey Clinical, a commercial partner-adjacent platform.
  • Total CE hours obtainable across the whole certificate pathway are not aggregated anywhere; they have to be added up module by module, and the retreat carries none.
