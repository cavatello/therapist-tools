Spiral Process Therapy

Psychedelic Process Training for Therapists

A six-day, $1,950 in-person ketamine-and-somatics intensive in Santa Cruz run by one LMFT, where the tuition includes two ketamine sessions for you as a student.

What it is

This is the smallest and cheapest of the trainings in this directory, and the most concretely about work a California therapist can legally do tomorrow. It is six days in person in Santa Cruz — two three-day weekends, 10am to 5pm, no virtual option — taught by Dmitry Vulfovich, LMFT, the founder and clinical director of Spiral Process Therapy, a Campbell/South Bay practice that offers ketamine-assisted psychotherapy. The stated content is the full arc of a psychedelic-assisted session: assessment and vetting, preparation and priming, in-session facilitation using experiential methods (movement, chair work, expressive arts, guided imagery), touch and attachment work, integration, and coordinating with the prescriber who actually writes the ketamine script.

The unusual feature is the tuition structure. $1,950 covers the training and, per the program's own page, 'includes ketamine medicine (2 sessions) for each student.' Because ketamine has to be routed through a medical prescriber, the program says refunds will not be granted except for a compelling medical emergency. A $450 deposit plus $50 processing fee is due at registration. There is no published payment-plan schedule beyond 'available per request.'

Eligibility is broad on paper and narrower in the fine print. The Psychedelic Process Training page says the course is 'open to all healing practitioners including coaches, shamans, nurses, doctors, licensed therapists and registered associates,' and that the organisation 'highly value[s]' applicants who have done its own Spiral Process Method (SPM) course or an equivalent experiential foundation such as Hakomi, IFS or Somatic Experiencing. The separate SPM course page states it more firmly: 'Completion of this course is also a prerequisite for the Psychedelic Process Training.' Those two sentences do not agree, and a prospective student should get the answer in writing before paying a non-refundable deposit. SPM itself is a separate 7-day, $1,750 course.

Continuing education is the other thing to pin down. Spiral Process Therapy is a CAMFT-approved CE sponsor (provider number 1000186) and awards 35 CE hours for the SPM course, explicitly meeting California BBS requirements for LMFT, LCSW, LPCC and LEP renewal. The Psychedelic Process Training page carries the same accommodations-and-grievances boilerplate a CE-approved course would, but states no CE hour count anywhere. Ask. Six days at roughly 6 instructional hours a day is about 36 hours of teaching, which would be meaningful CE if it is in fact approved.

What it lets you do

This is the section the programme’s own marketing is least specific about, so it runs before everything else. It was established against the programme’s own materials and against the two state registers that matter — Oregon’s approved-curriculum list and Colorado’s.

This does not change your licence or your scope of practiceIt is a credential from a school, not from a regulator. California’s Board of Behavioral Sciences neither recognises nor requires it, and holding it adds nothing to what you may lawfully do as an LMFT in this state.
With this you can
  • Practise ketamine-assisted psychotherapy in California as the therapist half of a two-role arrangement — you do the preparation, sit the session and do the integration, while a physician, NP or PA evaluates the client and prescribes the ketamine. Ketamine is an FDA-approved anaesthetic prescribed off-label for psychiatric indications; nothing about this training authorises you to prescribe or dispense it.
  • Offer psychedelic integration therapy in California. Integration is ordinary psychotherapy about an experience the client had elsewhere, and requires no special credential beyond your BBS licence.
  • Use the experiential, somatic and touch-based methods taught here inside your existing scope as an LMFT, LCSW, LPCC or psychologist, subject to your board's rules on touch and informed consent.
  • Have had two supervised ketamine sessions yourself, which is the sort of first-hand experience clinics and KAP practices ask about when hiring.
  • Plausibly claim California BBS continuing education — but only if this specific course is CE-approved. Spiral Process Therapy is a CAMFT-approved CE sponsor (provider 1000186) and awards 35 CE hours for its Spiral Process Method course; the Psychedelic Process Training page states no CE hours at all. Confirm before you assume.
You still cannot
  • Change your licence or scope of practice. This is a certificate from a private practice, not a credential from the California Board of Behavioral Sciences. The BBS does not recognise, require or register psychedelic-therapy certificates.
  • Prescribe, administer, supply or possess ketamine. That is the prescriber's role, and it stays the prescriber's role after this course.
  • Legally work with psilocybin or MDMA with clients in California. Psilocybin and psilocyn are Schedule I under California Health & Safety Code §11054(d)(18)-(19), and MDMA-assisted therapy has no FDA approval — the FDA issued Lykos Therapeutics a complete response letter in August 2024 and no MDMA product is approved as of August 2026.
  • Qualify for the Oregon psilocybin facilitator licence or the Colorado natural medicine facilitator licence. Spiral Process Therapy does not appear on the Oregon Health Authority's list of training programs with approved curriculum, nor on Colorado DORA's list of approved Natural Medicine Training Programs (both checked 6 August 2026).
  • Call yourself a 'psychedelic therapist' as a protected or regulated title. No such title exists in California.

Cost, length and who can apply

Cost$1,950 per participant for the September 2026 cohort; the program states this 'includes ketamine medicine (2 sessions) for each student.' $450 deposit plus $50 processing fee at registration, balance due before the start date. Payment plans 'available per request' (no published terms). Refunds are not granted except for a compelling medical emergency, because of the prescriber logistics. Travel and lodging for both Santa Cruz weekends are not included and not priced. The prerequisite Spiral Process Method course, if required, is a separate $1,750 plus $300 deposit and $50 processing fee.
Length6 days, delivered as two three-day weekends within one month (September 11-13 and 25-27, 2026)
HoursSix days at 10am-5pm including a one-hour lunch and two 15-minute breaks — roughly 5.5 instructional hours per day, about 33 hours total. The program does not publish an official hour count or a CE hour count for this course.
FormatIn person only, in Santa Cruz, California. 'Virtual attendance is not available – this is an in-person immersive program.' Both weekends require accommodation for out-of-town participants.
WhereSanta Cruz, CA — fully in person
Who can applyStated as open to 'all healing practitioners including coaches, shamans, nurses, doctors, licensed therapists and registered associates (LMFT, LCSW, MFT, psychologists, and equivalents).' The training page says the program 'highly value[s]' completion of its own Spiral Process Method course or an equivalent experiential foundation (Hakomi, IFS, Somatic Experiencing); the SPM course page states outright that SPM completion 'is also a prerequisite for the Psychedelic Process Training.' Registration deadline for the September 2026 cohort is 21 August 2026.

Who teaches it

Faculty is a large part of what these programmes actually sell, and it changes between cohorts — check the current list before you decide on this basis.

  • Dmitry Vulfovich, LMFT — founder, clinical director and the sole named instructor. Master's thesis from John F. Kennedy University in Ethnobotanical Assisted Psychotherapy; completed the MAPS PBC MDMA-Assisted Therapy Education Program (50 hours); trained directly by Don and Carol Hadlock in foundational and advanced Process Therapy including a year-long one-way-mirror experiential therapy program; 190 hours of dance movement therapy training with Bonnie Bernstein; introductory Hakomi. He is described on the site as a Process Therapy Master Teacher and a Zen teacher in the Vipassana tradition.
  • Nadia Vulfovich — program administrator and the contact for eligibility, prerequisites, accommodations and grievances. Not listed as teaching faculty.
  • No other named instructors, guest faculty or co-facilitators are published for this course.

What I could not establish

These are the things I looked for and could not establish. Take them to the programme, and notice how quickly and precisely they answer.

  • Cohort size. Not published anywhere on the site for this course. The related SPM course page refers only to a 'limited in-person learning space model.'
  • CE hours for this specific course. The organisation is a CAMFT-approved CE sponsor and publishes 35 CE hours for its SPM course, but the Psychedelic Process Training page states no CE hour figure.
  • Whether the Spiral Process Method course is a hard prerequisite. The two site pages contradict each other.
  • Whether the two included ketamine sessions require a separate prescriber evaluation fee for the student, and whether the ketamine is administered at the training venue or elsewhere.
  • Any independent review, journalism, alumni account, Trustpilot listing or forum discussion of this specific training. None found. Reddit is unreachable from this environment and general web-search engines were blocked, so this is a genuine gap rather than a finding of absence.
  • Any named faculty other than the founder, and whether the program has run before (no past-cohort dates, alumni count or testimonials are published).
  • Any YouTube video about this specific training. Spiral Process Therapy has a YouTube channel with general psychotherapy talks by Dmitry Vulfovich, but nothing found about the Psychedelic Process Training.
