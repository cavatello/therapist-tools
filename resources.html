Where numbers come from

The sources behind the tools here

Every figure in the calculators on this site traces to one of these. They are listed so you can check the work, or build your own.

MIT Living Wage CalculatorA county-specific floor for what you actually need to earn, rather than a national average. California's living wage for one adult with no children is $30.48 an hour; data last updated 15 February 2026. Open →
BLS wage data by occupationThe authoritative wage survey. Note BLS retired its static per-occupation pages after May 2023, so current figures have to be read from the interactive tool. Open →
Median pay — marriage and family therapists$63,780 median, May 2024, with employment projected to grow 13% from 2024 to 2034. Open →
Median pay — counselors$59,190 a year, or $28.46 an hour, May 2024. Open →
Median pay — social workers$61,330 median overall; healthcare social workers $68,090 and mental health and substance abuse social workers $60,060, May 2024. Open →
California payroll tax ratesIf you elect S-corp treatment and put yourself on payroll, these come out of your own cheque. SDI is 1.3% for 2026 with no wage cap — the cap was removed in 2024, so any older model that caps it is wrong. Open →
California tax rate schedulesThe bracket tables the state calculation should be built from, straight from FTB. Re-check each January. Open →
