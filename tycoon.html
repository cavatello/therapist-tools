Therapy Tycoon is a game, not a financial planner — but the numbers underneath it are real ones. Insurance reimbursement for a 53-minute session (CPT 90837) uses California figures: Kaiser $150, Anthem/BCBS $123, Optum $121, Medicare $125, Aetna $113, Medi-Cal $98, Cigna $80. Private-pay fees use the Bay Area range of $180–350. A full caseload is 20–25 clients/week, roughly 30 minutes of unpaid admin follows each clinical hour, and outside billing costs 7–11% of collections. Entity choice is Sole Proprietorship vs. a California Professional Corporation with an S-corp election — California-licensed MFTs cannot form an LLC (Cal. Corp. Code §17701.04(e)). For the real math on your own practice, use the Practice Income Planner or read the field notes on California therapy rates.
